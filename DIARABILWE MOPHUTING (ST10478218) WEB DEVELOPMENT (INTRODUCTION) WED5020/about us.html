About Rayton Bakery

Who We Are

Rayton Bakery is a local bakery focused on providing fresh, delicious and quality baked products to our customers.

We believe that good baking is about more than just food. It is about bringing people together and creating special moments for families, friends and communities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide fresh and affordable baked products while giving our customers excellent service.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted bakery in the Rayton community, known for quality, freshness and friendly service.
